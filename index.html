🍽️ Restaurants 🍦 Ice Cream 🍔 Burgers

01 Restaurants 🍽️

There are lots of options, but the most popular ones are these two:

Fraps 🍺 Bar & restaurant 💸 Cheap

It work both as bar and restaurant, One of the cheapest on the zone!!!

Contrato ⭐ Yummiest 💰 Pricey

while their prices are a lot higher than Fraps, the food and the service is almost at the level. Prepare yourself for the yummiest food in the city! (at least for us).

02 Ice Cream Shops 🍦

After a heavy meal, why not an ice cream to refresh the body?

Grido 🍨 Classic 💸 Cheap

offers a classic and cheap option, beware of the lactose!

Nevado ❤️ Local favourite ⭐ Quality

some of the favourites of the citizens. If you want quality, this is your place!!!

03 Burguer Shops 🍔

What’s better than a happy burger dinner with the family?

BGR 🥡 Takeaway 🍟 Fast food

specialized in fast food, you can take your order home and taste some good burgers.

LP 🧑‍🍳 Artesanal 🎒 Teen favourite

loved by teenagers, overrated for others, they do artesanal burgers. Better to give it a shot to see if it’s the best in the area or not.
